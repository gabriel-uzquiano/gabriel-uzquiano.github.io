The electronic textbook for the course, Modal Logic, will be available online. Further resources include:&nbsp;

- Cresswell and Hughes. A New Introduction to Modal Logic. Taylor & Francis, 1996. <br/>NB. Hughes and Cresswell use the symbol L for $$\Box$$ and M for $$\Diamond$$.
