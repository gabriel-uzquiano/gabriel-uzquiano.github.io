This is the website for [PHIL 452. Modal Logic (Fall 2026)](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/452/Syllabus.pdf). 

## start here

1. Read the [syllabus](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/452/Syllabus.pdf).

2. Bookmark the electronic textbook [Modal Logic](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/modal-logic/). Each topic listed in the schedule
below links to the relevant section.

3. Bookmark the [google form](https://forms.gle/Hqr19Q35dCk6zKHQA) you will use to sign up for a problem presentation.

4. Skim the [guidelines for the final project](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/452/Guidelines-for-the-Final-Project.pdf). 
Your proposal is due on Monday, November 11, and you will submit your final project by Wednesday, December 12.
