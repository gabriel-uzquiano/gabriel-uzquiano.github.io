This is the website for [PHIL 220. Introduction to Logic (Fall 2026)](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/220/Syllabus.pdf).

## start here

1. Read the [syllabus](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/220/Syllabus.pdf).

2. Create a [Carnap](https://carnap.io/auth/login) account and enroll in [USC Introduction to Logic Fall 2026](https://carnap.io/enroll/USC%20Introduction%20to%20Logic,%20Fall%202026).<br/>
Your assignments will be visible in your [user page](https://carnap.io/user/) on [Carnap](https://carnap.io/). 

3. Before the first online assignment, skim [How to Type Logical Symbols in Carnap](https://carnap.io/shared/uzquiano@usc.edu/how%20to%20type%20logical%20symbols%20in%20Carnap.md).

4. Find out which discussion section you are in, and note your section leader's email address.
