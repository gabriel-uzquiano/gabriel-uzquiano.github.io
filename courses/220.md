This is the website for [PHIL 220. Introduction to Logic (Fall 2026)](https://gabriel-uzquiano.github.io/220/Syllabus.pdf).

The electronic textbook for this course, Introduction to Logic, will be available online. Sections of the book are available from the online platform [USC Logic Web](https://dornsife.usc.edu/usclogicweb), which we will use as a companion to the course.

Assignments will be visible in your [user page](https://carnap.io/user/) on [Carnap](https://carnap.io/). You will find a suite of problems and solutions for different parts of the course in [USC Logic Web](https://dornsife.usc.edu/usclogicweb).

The schedule below includes links to lecture notes, online assignments, and other materials such as practice tests and solutions.

Make an appointment for online office hours.
